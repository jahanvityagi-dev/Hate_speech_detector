Title: Twitter Rules on Hateful Conduct

Source: https://help.twitter.com/en/rules-and-policies/hateful-conduct-policy

Policy Definition:
Twitter prohibits the promotion of violence, threats, or harassment against people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, religious affiliation, age, disability, or serious disease.

Prohibited Behaviors:
Includes inciting fear, spreading harmful stereotypes, and dehumanizing language.

Enforcement Measures:
Violations may lead to content removal, temporary account locks, or permanent suspension.

Reporting Tools:
Users can report hateful conduct, which Twitter reviews for potential action.

Policy Updates:
Twitter periodically updates its policies to address emerging issues and societal changes.
