Title: Meta's Community Standards on Hate Speech

Source: https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/hateful-conduct/

Definition:
Hate speech includes direct attacks on people based on protected characteristics such as race, ethnicity, national origin, religious affiliation, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, and serious disease.

Policy Scope:
Prohibits violent or dehumanizing speech, harmful stereotypes, and content promoting exclusion or segregation.

Enforcement Actions:
Content violating these standards may be removed, and repeat offenders may face restrictions or account deactivation.

Recent Updates:
Meta has adjusted its policies to balance free expression with safety, including changes to enforcement practices and definitions.

User Tools:
Provides options for users to report hate speech and access support resources.
